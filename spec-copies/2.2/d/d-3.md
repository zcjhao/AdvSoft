## 4. Performance

The check opens one subscriber per node (three different users, all members of one
channel), then posts 90 messages at the same time: 3 writer threads per node, 10 messages
each. Latency is measured per subscriber from just before each POST is sent to the moment
its `message.created` frame arrives.

4.1 **All writes succeed.** All 90 posts return `201` within 5 seconds each.

4.2 **Dense.** Every subscriber sees exactly the 90 new `event_id`s, head + 1 to head + 90,
    each once.

4.3 **Median latency.** For every subscriber, the median latency is at most 300 ms.

4.4 **p95 latency.** For every subscriber, the 95th percentile latency is at most 800 ms.

4.5 **Missing events.** Within 5 seconds after the last post, no subscriber is missing more
    than 1% of the 90 events it should get.

Every request must answer within 5 seconds.
