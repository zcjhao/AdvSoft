## 7. Messages

**Rows.** `message-list` shows the current channel's top-level messages, oldest at the top
and newest at the bottom. Each row is `data-testid="message"` with `data-message-id="<id>"`
and holds: `message-avatar` (an element with the author's initial on a coloured
background), `message-author` (display name), `message-time` (a human-readable time such as
`10:42 AM`, with the ISO time in its `title`), and `message-body` (the body text). An edited
message also shows `message-edited` with the text `(edited)`. A message with replies shows
`reply-count` with the text `1 reply` / `N replies`.

**Composer.** `message-input` plus `send-btn` (and Enter) posts to the current channel and
clears the input. Every hook the check types into (`message-input`, `message-edit-input`,
`thread-input`) must itself be the `<input>` or `<textarea>`, not a wrapper around one.
A blank or whitespace-only input posts nothing.

**Hover toolbar.** Hovering a row shows its toolbar (it may stay in the DOM but hidden
until `:hover`): `open-thread-btn`, `reaction-button`, and for the author's own messages
`message-edit-btn` and `message-delete-btn`. Edit turns the body into `message-edit-input`
(pre-filled); Enter or `message-edit-save` saves with `PATCH`, Escape cancels. Delete calls
`DELETE` at once, with no `confirm()` dialog, and the row disappears.

**Threads.** `open-thread-btn` shows `thread-panel` as a right-hand pane (to the right of
`message-list`, which stays visible). It shows the parent and its replies, each reply as
`data-testid="thread-message"`. `thread-input` plus `thread-send` posts a reply
(`parent_id`); the reply appears in the panel and the parent's `reply-count` updates.
`close-thread` hides the panel. Lay the page out as one flex row (`sidebar`, the channel
column with `message-list`, then `thread-panel`), so the panel sits beside the list rather
than below it.

**Reactions.** `reaction-button` opens `emoji-picker`, a grid of at least 8 buttons, each
`data-testid="emoji-option"` with `data-emoji="<emoji>"` and the emoji as its text. Never use
`prompt()`. Clicking an option adds that reaction (`POST`) and closes the picker; the row
then shows `reaction-chip` with `data-emoji="<emoji>"`, the emoji and its count. Clicking
your own chip removes your reaction.

**Live updates.** A message posted by someone else in the current channel appears within
3 seconds without a reload (WebSocket).

7.1 **Post.** Typing a message and clicking `send-btn` adds a row whose `message-body` is the
    text, with visible `message-avatar`, `message-author` and non-empty `message-time`.

7.2 **No blank post.** Clicking `send-btn` with an empty or whitespace input adds no row.

7.3 **Edit.** Hover the row, click `message-edit-btn`, replace the text in
    `message-edit-input`, press Enter: `message-body` shows the new text and
    `message-edited` is visible.

7.4 **Survives reload.** After a hard reload, the edited body and `message-edited` are still
    shown.

7.5 **Delete.** Hover a second message, click `message-delete-btn`: its row disappears and
    stays gone after a reload.

7.6 **Thread.** Hover the first message, click `open-thread-btn`: `thread-panel` is visible
    and to the right of `message-list`. Post a reply with `thread-input` and `thread-send`:
    a `thread-message` with the reply text appears and the parent row's `reply-count` shows
    `1 reply`. `close-thread` hides the panel.

7.7 **Reaction.** Hover the first message, click `reaction-button`: `emoji-picker` shows at
    least 8 `emoji-option`s (no `prompt()` dialog). Clicking one adds a `reaction-chip` with
    count `1` on that row.

7.8 **Live.** A message posted by another user through the API appears in the open channel
    within 3 seconds without a reload.

7.9 **Layout.** `sidebar` is left of `message-list`, and its computed background colour is
    neither white nor transparent; `workspace-header` and `channel-title` are visible.

Every request must answer within 5 seconds.
