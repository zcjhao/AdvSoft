## 7. User groups

Routes under `/api/workspaces/{slug}/groups`. Only workspace members may use them
(non-member → `403`, unknown workspace → `404`). `handle` is 1 to 32 characters of
lowercase letters, digits, `-` and `_`; it is unique within the workspace.

7.1 **Create.** `POST /api/workspaces/{slug}/groups`
    `{"handle": "designers", "name": "Design team", "user_ids": [<bob>, <carol>]}` from any
    workspace member (a plain `member` is enough) → `201 {group: {id, handle, name,
    member_user_ids}}` with `member_user_ids` the ids sent (`user_ids` may be omitted:
    empty group).

7.2 **List.** `GET /api/workspaces/{slug}/groups` (any node) → `200 {groups: [GroupObj]}`
    holding the new group. A user who is not in the workspace → `403`. No token → `401`.

7.3 **Validation.** A duplicate handle in the same workspace → `409`. A bad handle
    (`"bad handle"`, `""`) or a missing `name` → `400`. A `user_ids` entry that is not a
    member of the workspace → `400`.

7.4 **Update.** `PATCH /api/workspaces/{slug}/groups/{handle}` with any of
    `{name, user_ids}` from the creator → `200 {group}` with the new values (`user_ids`
    replaces the whole member list). From the workspace owner → `200`. From another plain
    member → `403`. Unknown handle → `404`.

7.5 **Delete.** `DELETE /api/workspaces/{slug}/groups/{handle}` from another plain member →
    `403`. From the creator (or owner) → `200 {"deleted": true}`; calling it again (the group
    is gone) → `200 {"deleted": true}` again. The group is no longer listed.

## 8. Mentions: @group-handle and @here

A message's `mentions` is the deduplicated list of user ids from all of these forms. A form
matches only at the start of the body or after a character that is not a letter, digit,
`_`, `.` or `@`, and ends at the first character that is not a letter, digit, `_` or `-`.

- `@<username>`: that user, if they are a member of the channel's workspace.
- `@channel`: every member of the channel.
- `@<group-handle>` (a group of the channel's workspace): the group members **who are also
  members of the channel**.
- `@here`: the channel members who **currently have an open WebSocket** on any node.

The author is never in `mentions`, and a message never raises the author's own
`mention_count`. Each mentioned user's `mention_count` (read state, if built) goes up by 1
per message, however many forms mention them.

8.1 **Group.** The group is alice, bob, carol and dave; the channel has alice, bob and carol
    (not dave). Alice posts `"hi @designers"` → `mentions` is bob and carol only.

8.2 **@here.** Bob has an open WebSocket (on another node, not subscribed to anything),
    carol has none. Alice posts `"@here standup"` → `mentions` is bob only.

8.3 **Gone offline.** Within 2 seconds after bob's socket closes, `"@here again"` no longer
    mentions bob.

8.4 **Deduplicated.** `"@bob @designers @here"` lists every id once.

8.5 **Never self.** Alice is in the group and online; `"@designers @here @<alice>"` from
    alice does not list alice. If read state exists, alice's `mention_count` in that channel
    does not change.

8.6 **Unknown handle.** `"@nosuchgroup hi"` mentions nobody and still posts (`201`).

Every request must answer within 5 seconds.
